# Luna's Bouquet — Project Constitution

## What this is
A brand-new marketing website for **Luna's Bouquet**, a Phoenix-area florist. There is a live
site today (lunasbouquet.com, a Wix Studio build) but the owner dislikes the look and feel.
We are rebuilding it from scratch with a luxury-editorial aesthetic.

## Who the florist is
- **Ahnaleigh ("Annie")** — the founder. She grew up in foster care, discovered floral design
  at 15, and is building this business to save for her first car, matched by the Arizona Friends
  of Foster Children Foundation's *Keys to Success* program.
- This story is real and it matters. It's handled with dignity, never as a pity pitch.
- Ties into Christine's Butterfly Effect Foundation world (foster-youth mentorship).

## What the business sells
- **Holiday arrangements** — Valentine's Day, Easter, Mother's Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas.
- **Events** — weddings, corporate events, parties, gatherings, meetings.
- **Pricing (CONFIRMED by Christine, 2026-07-23):**
  - Seasonal / holiday arrangements: **$125 each** (includes vase + free Phoenix-metro delivery).
  - One-off / custom orders: **$375 minimum**.
  - Events: quote-based (treat as custom).
- Quarterly flower membership.
- Free delivery within Phoenix Metro Area including Scottsdale. Minimum 48 hours notice.
- Payment: Venmo, Zelle, check, cash (arranged after order submission).

## Brand aspiration
- **McQueens London-level luxury.** Editorial, refined, warm. See assets/source/mcqueen-inspiration.png.
- Existing logo: "LUNA'S BOUQUET" serif LB monogram + line-art rose, warm gold on cream.
- Hero graphic (MidJourney): airy cream/peach/coral garden-rose arrangement with cream negative
  space on the left for a headline overlay. assets/source/hero.png.
- Home-page layout inspiration: full-bleed floral hero, centered logo "tab" in nav.
  assets/source/homepage-inspiration.png.

## What we're building (scope)
1. Luxury marketing site (home, about/story, offerings, testimonials, contact).
2. **Order form** — recreate the fields from Annie's existing Google Form
   (https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdwaSfzmId76janXCb0-Bj8onz4Ym2LLhgXcFq2CowFHnCDVg/viewform).
3. **Supabase** database to receive & store orders.
4. **Web3Forms** so Annie is emailed the moment an order comes in.
5. Branding: derive main colors from the McQueen inspiration + hero, tie to logo. Confirm colors
   with Christine via a mark mockup + a color artifact. Choose a font.
